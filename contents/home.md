I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Lanzhou Jiaotong University. My research interests include spatio-temporal forecasting, traffic intelligence, multimodal learning, and speech-driven 3D digital humans.

Currently, I am working on MEMD-guided traffic flow forecasting, large-model-enhanced spatio-temporal prediction, and speech-driven full-body motion generation. My recent work explores how decomposition-guided temporal representations and branch-specific neural architectures can improve robust multi-step forecasting under heterogeneous traffic dynamics.
