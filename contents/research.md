#### Spatio-Temporal Traffic Forecasting

My current research focuses on robust multi-step traffic flow forecasting under heterogeneous temporal dynamics. I study decomposition-guided temporal representation learning, where multivariate empirical mode decomposition is used to construct aligned trend and residual representations before neural forecasting.

Representative topics:

- MEMD-guided trend-residual reconstruction
- Branch-specific recurrent forecasting networks
- Dynamic spatial dependency learning for traffic networks
- Large-language-model-enhanced traffic forecasting

#### Speech-Driven 3D Digital Humans

I am also involved in research on speech-driven full-body motion generation and personalized avatar rendering. My work includes reproducing and improving recent models such as TalkSHOW, EMAGE, and GUAVA, with attention to speech-motion alignment, body-part coordination, and real-time personalized rendering.

Representative topics:

- Speech-to-motion generation
- Personalized 3D avatar rendering
- Multimodal alignment and temporal consistency

#### Edge AI for Railway Inspection

I lead a national undergraduate innovation project on air-ground collaborative railway yard inspection. The project investigates an edge-AI-centered inspection pipeline that integrates visual perception, real-time transmission, knowledge-augmented decision support, and digital twin management for railway freight safety scenarios.
